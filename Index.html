About Us
Built in Ottawa.
Backed by one person.

CoreCraft Computers is a home-based custom PC building business in Ottawa, Ontario. Founded with a genuine passion for hardware and a belief that quality computers should be more accessible, CoreCraft assembles machines for gamers, students, creators, and professionals.

Every build is put together by hand, tested before it ships, and backed by a personal warranty. No call centres, no waiting on hold — just a builder who knows computers and stands behind their work.

Your money goes into the parts that matter, not into someone’s brand recognition budget.

  • Stress-tested before every shipment
  • Parts from Canada Computers, Memory Express & Newegg CA
  • Progress photos sent during every build
  • Custom quotes turned around within 24 hours
  • Discounted upgrade service for returning customers
Values & Standards
A business you can rely on.
Clear legal, ethical, and environmental commitments — published here so there are no surprises.
/01

Legal Compliance

Registered Ontario sole proprietorship. Every order comes with a written 90-day labour warranty. Payments processed securely through Shopify Payments — card data never stored locally.

/02

Honest Pricing

Fully transparent component breakdowns on every build. No counterfeit or grey-market parts. Performance claims based on real benchmark data, not marketing copy.

/03

Fair Warranty Handling

If a part fails within the warranty window due to an assembly error, we repair or replace it at no cost. No arguments, no runaround.

/04

E-Waste Responsibility

Defective or returned parts that can’t be reused are dropped off at a local e-waste recycling facility. Packaging uses recyclable cardboard wherever possible.

/05

Data Privacy

We collect only what’s needed to process your order. Customer data is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

/06

Community

We participate in the Ottawa tech community through online forums. As the business grows we plan to sponsor local gaming events and give back.
