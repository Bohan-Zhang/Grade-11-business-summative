About Us
Built in Ottawa.
Handled personally.

CoreCraft Computers is a small custom PC shop based in Ottawa, Ontario. We build practical, well-specced desktops for gamers, students, creators, and professionals who want a better option than an off-the-shelf pre-built.

Every system is assembled by hand, tested before delivery, and backed by a 90-day labour warranty. If you have a question, you talk to the person who built the PC.

The goal is simple: spend more of your budget on the parts that affect performance and less on branding, filler software, and generic support scripts.

  • Stress-tested before every shipment
  • Parts from Canada Computers, Memory Express & Newegg CA
  • Progress photos sent during every build
  • Custom quotes turned around within 24 hours
  • Discounted upgrade service for returning customers
Values & Standards
A business you can rely on.
Clear policies for pricing, privacy, warranty handling, and responsible parts disposal.
Legal

Legal Compliance

CoreCraft operates as an Ontario sole proprietorship. Every order includes a written 90-day labour warranty, and payments are processed securely through Shopify Payments. Card data is never stored locally.

Pricing

Honest Pricing

Every quote includes a clear component breakdown. Parts are sourced from trusted Canadian retailers, and performance expectations are based on real benchmark data.

Warranty

Fair Warranty Handling

If an issue is caused by assembly within the warranty window, we repair it at no labour cost. We keep the process direct and reasonable.

Env.

E-Waste Responsibility

Defective or returned parts that can’t be reused are dropped off at a local e-waste recycling facility. Packaging uses recyclable cardboard and paper cushioning wherever possible.

Privacy

Data Privacy

We collect only what’s needed to process your order. Customer data is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Local

Community

CoreCraft is part of the Ottawa tech and gaming community online, with plans to support local events as the business grows.
